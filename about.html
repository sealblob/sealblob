OUR STORY

A space for artists, ideas and imagination.

Artemis Gallery was founded with a simple belief: art should invite people to pause, question and feel.

We represent a diverse group of contemporary artists whose practices challenge conventional perspectives and explore the complexities of modern life.

From painting and sculpture to photography and mixed media, our exhibitions create a meeting point between artists and audiences.
